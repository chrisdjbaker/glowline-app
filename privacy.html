Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy covers the Glowline app for iPhone and iPad (“the app”).

We don’t collect your data

The developer does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts of its own, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The developer runs no servers.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, Apple handles matchmaking, invitations, leaderboards and achievements under Apple's privacy policy. During an online match, the game sends moves and your chosen name and trail color to the other players in the match through Game Center. Nothing is sent to the developer.

iCloud

Your progress (rank, points, badges, unlocked colors, settings and your best Daily Arena run) is saved on your device and in your own iCloud account, so it comes back if you reinstall. The developer cannot see it.

Challenge links

When you share a Daily Arena challenge, the link contains your chosen name, trail color, score and the moves of your run, so your friend can race your ghost. It is only sent where you choose to share it.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
